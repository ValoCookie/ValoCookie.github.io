osu!StreamDeck

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026.

What the app uses

osu!StreamDeck can connect to Twitch and osu! so it can receive beatmap requests, send chat replies, identify the linked osu! account and run the stream features you turn on. Patreon linking is optional and is only used to verify supporter status.

Account data

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle Twitch usernames and OAuth tokens, an osu! username, relay session credentials and optional Patreon link identifiers. These are used to provide the connected features you asked for.

Local storage

App settings and connection information are stored locally on your Windows PC so you do not have to reconnect everything every time the app starts. Disconnecting an account removes or invalidates the related connection where supported.

ValoCookie relay

Some connected features communicate with a ValoCookie-operated relay. The data sent depends on the feature and can include the session or account information needed to complete authentication, refresh a connection, verify supporter status or deliver a requested action.

Anonymous diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are optional and off unless you enable them. When enabled, the app may send active-install and crash fingerprints used to understand failures and usage health. The diagnostic option is designed not to include usernames, Twitch chat, authentication tokens or map requests.

Bug reports

The in-app diagnostics tool can prepare technical information for a bug report. It redacts Twitch tokens, relay tokens and passwords. You choose whether to copy or submit that information.

Third-party services

Twitch, osu!, Patreon, GitHub and other services used by a feature process data under their own privacy policies. osu!StreamDeck is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those services.

Your choices

You can use the app without optional Patreon linking or anonymous diagnostics. You can disconnect Twitch and osu!-related connections from the app. If you stop using osu!StreamDeck, you can also remove its local settings from your PC.

Questions

For privacy or support questions, use the osu!StreamDeck support page. Please do not post passwords, OAuth tokens or other secrets in a public issue.
