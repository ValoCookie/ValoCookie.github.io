Support

Something broke? very possible. Here's where to report it without throwing account secrets onto the internet.

Bug reports

Use the BUG REPORT button inside osu!StreamDeck when possible. It can prepare diagnostics that make the problem much easier to trace.

Open GitHub Issues →

Include this stuff

Tell me what you were doing, what you expected to happen, what actually happened and the osu!StreamDeck version you are using. Screenshots are extremely useful when the UI is being cursed.

Do not post secrets

Do not include Twitch OAuth tokens, relay tokens, Patreon link secrets, passwords or other private credentials. The built-in diagnostics are designed to redact authentication tokens.
