WHAT IT DOES

Run the boring checks before Go Live.

StreamFlight keeps stream preparation in one place so setup issues are easier to catch before the broadcast starts.

OBS checks

Surface whether OBS is running and keep OBS-related preflight actions close to the rest of your setup.

Windows health

Flag things such as a pending Windows restart so they do not become a surprise ten minutes into a stream.

Test recording

Keep a deliberate test-recording step in the workflow instead of discovering broken audio after going live.

Stream checklist

Use one workspace for the recurring steps you personally want to verify before every stream.

Optional osu! support

If osu!stable or osu!lazer is relevant, StreamFlight can introduce the companion osu!Requests app without making osu! part of the experience for everyone else.

Standalone by design

StreamFlight does not require osu!Requests. The integration is optional and the app remains useful for streamers who never touch osu!.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Does StreamFlight start my stream for me?

No. It is a preparation and health-check workspace. It helps you verify the setup; it does not press Go Live on your behalf.

Do I need osu!Requests?

No. osu!Requests is an optional companion for osu! streamers. StreamFlight works as a general streaming utility on its own.

Why would StreamFlight mention osu!Requests?

The introduction is only relevant when osu! is detected, so non-osu! streamers are not pushed toward an unrelated tool.

PLAY osu!?

Let chat send the maps.

osu!Requests handles Twitch beatmap requests, queue rules, filters, history, !np and OBS request overlays.
