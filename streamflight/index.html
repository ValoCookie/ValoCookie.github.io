How i use it

i pick the profile, start the apps, open the websites, go through the checklist and then start stream. thats basically it. the point is just to stop doing the same boring setup manually every time.

FAQ

Does StreamFlight press Go Live?

No. It gets the stuff around the stream ready. It does not start the broadcast for you.

Can it keep helper apps hidden?

Yep. Background helpers can launch hidden and you can give them a hotkey to bring them back when needed.

Can I have different setups for different games?

Yep. That is what profiles are for.

Do I need osu!StreamDeck?

Nope. StreamFlight works by itself.
